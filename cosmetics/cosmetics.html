Cosmetic Wardrobe

Select a hat or backpack to equip it. Select the same item again to remove it. You can wear one hat and one backpack at a time.

You can freely interact with the 3d model by left click to rotate it, scroll to zoom and right click to moving around

The price counter shows the combined price of your selected items in coins and Mitty coins. Items with no coin or Mitty coin price are marked Exclusive in the item list.
