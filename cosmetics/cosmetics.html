Cosmetic Wardrobe

Select a hat or backpack to equip it. Select the same item again to remove it. You can wear one hat and one backpack at a time.

You can freely interact with the 3D model by left-clicking to rotate it, scroll to zoom and right click to move around.

The price counter displays the combined price of your selected items in Orth Coins and Mitty coins. Certain items can only be obtained through limited-time events; Those are marked as exclusive.

The cosmestics that aren't yet available are likely still locked behind copyright; We are actively working to try and bring you these cosmetics without commiting any violations.
