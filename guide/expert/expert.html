Farm Tips

Farming in abyss is quite different from other server. You cant build any type of farm in the abyss.

The best way to farm is try to focus in 1 place that have the high rate of mobs and focus on one type of mob. For example, Abyssal Snails is the best money source in the abyss, so you will wanna try your best to farm as many snails as possible, you can try to use nature grass platform, but that is violated the rule so im not gonna recommend that.

Try to arrange your inventory, you can try to arrage it with the rate of 2 casual drops backpack and 3 for farming drops backpack.

Wear Frayed Murmur, it give you invisiblity so you can ignore some hostile mob. ( you can try wear no armor like Anvly if you want )

"Doomscrolling while farming" - Anvly.
