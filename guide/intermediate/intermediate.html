Mining

Mining is a crucial part to progess in this server. Start with some common knowledge about mining.

1. Ore in abyss is quite different from other server, keep mining and ore will spawn. Each layer rate is different, but usually the deeper you go the better ore you will get. (ofc with some exception like L4 actually have more iron than L5)

2. Try to brew haste potion, im not lying, having haste is game changing, it will make your diamond progession way way easier and faster. If you get relic like drill ( Atlas Dignitas), welp you dont need to read this mining section anymore.

3. It usually my own experience, but focus on getting fortune 3 and efficiency 4 pickaxe, even if its iron scrap. Since you will be mining a lot at the early game, having these is really helpful.

4. You can read this one for Ore information: Ore Guide

Level up your gear

Leveling up your gear is also not only necessary for progession, but also for safer descending.

Focus on stacking up your ore and mob drop. At least gather the amount of mob drop that worth around at least 96 coins ( for full set of mending and your sword and pickaxe).

Once you got full set of diamond, try to gather your netherite template material:
- Makihige Shell (for template ).
- Stingerhead Eyes ( for template ).
- Hasofu Eyes ( for template ).
- Tachitakana Claw ( for template ).
- Silkfang Eyes ( for template ).
- Tesuchi Scales ( for template ).


Once you got your template, use them with the netherite ingot that can be crafted with 4 Stingerhead Scraps and 4 Gold Ingots.

After that, you can try to focus on farming praying skeleton to get your strong relic, which is the late game content of this server. ( im gonna be fair, relic aint a good late game content tho :D).

You can read this for your relic farming: Skeleton Guide
